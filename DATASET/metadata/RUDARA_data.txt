📁 Deepfake Video Dataset Overview

This dataset is a curated combination of videos from 3 popular deepfake datasets, combined to build a stronger training/test environment.

Sources:
- FaceForensics++: 2000 videos
- Deepfake Detection Challenge (DFDC): 3000 videos
- Celeb-DF: 1000 videos

📦 Total: 6000 videos
